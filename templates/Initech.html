INITECH

NASDAQ : INT
CEO : Greg Mendel
HQ : Portland, USA
No. Of Employees : 1,500,000

Initech is an American multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. It has been referred to as one of the most influential economic and cultural forces in the world, and is one of the world's most valuable brands. It was founded by Shawn Mendel in 1986.
