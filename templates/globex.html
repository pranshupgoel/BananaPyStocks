GLOBEX CORPORATION

NASDAQ : GLBX
CEO : Jonathan Xavier (1977-present)
HQ : Chicago, USA
No. Of Employees : 164,000

Globex Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Chicago, United States. It was founded by Jonathan Xavier and Xander Davis in 1977. It began largely as a company that sold printers and scanners, before moving on to computers and mobile phones.
