CLIFTON INDUSTRIES

NYSE : CLF
CEO : Richard Clifton
HQ : Houston, USA
No. Of Employees : 99000

Clifton is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company. It designs and manufactures electric vehicles, battery energy storage from home to grid-scale, solar panels and solar roof tiles, and related products and services. It is one of the world's most valuable companies and is, as of 2022, the world's most valuable automaker.
